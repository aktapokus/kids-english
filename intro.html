Aktapokus Kids English: a two-minute introduction

What? A free English vocabulary, listening and speaking app for children in grades 2–6. It opens in the browser on phones, tablets and smart boards; nothing to install.

Who makes it? Not a company or an institution: I am developing the app as a volunteer, in my free time. Nothing is sold.

Why? My aim is equal opportunity: access to a good learning tool should not depend on a family's budget. English is the first step; in the same spirit, I would like to build free tools for primary science, maths and coding too, and grow them into a learning ecosystem.

  • App: https://aktapokus.github.io/kids-english/
  • Teacher panel: https://aktapokus.github.io/kids-english/teacher.html
  • Full guide: https://aktapokus.github.io/kids-english/guide.html

What is it based on?

  • The Turkish Ministry of Education English curriculum (Maarif Model) and the Cambridge YLE word lists. British English is used.
  • Every word is taught with a picture + audio + example sentence.
  • Words come back again and again in different but similar sentences.
  • At primary level, grammar rules are not memorised; structures are learned from examples.
  • No account, no ads, no trackers.

What does the child do?

Each episode has 4–6 words. The steps come in order:

👀 Discover → ❓ Listen & choose → 🎤 Speak → 🧩 Build the sentence → 🔤 Complete the letters

  • Adventure: A space journey. Each station is a grade (Moon = grade 2, Mars = grade 3 … Galaxy = grade 6).
  • Library: Free practice. It includes:
    • words by topic;
    • days, months, seasons;
    • question words, opposites;
    • verb twins (go–went), A2 topics;
    • stories read aloud;
    • Sing & Move (chants and "Aktapokus Says").
  • Words the child gets wrong come back automatically on later days.

What can a teacher do?

🏫 Create a classCreate a class in the teacher panel and give the code to the students.
📊 Follow progressWho practised how much, which topics they finished, and which words they found hardest. There is also a view by MEB curriculum units.
🎯 Weekly taskChoose topics, add a note and a due date. The task appears on the student's home screen.
🧑‍🏫 Classroom modeBig pictures on the smart board and a two-team quiz game. Opens only from the teacher panel.
🖨️ PrintablesWord cards, bingo, worksheets (with an answer key).
⚑ Report a problemIf you see a wrong picture or sentence, report it with one tap.

The teacher panel is currently in Turkish; the app itself can be switched to English.

What does a parent see?

In the Parent Area inside the app:

  • the child's weekly practice and the words they found hard;
  • a weekly summary: words learned this week and 3 sentences to say together at home;
  • session length ("Continue, or tomorrow?");
  • backup.

Offline use and updates

  • It works offline. Open the app once on a good connection, wait a few minutes and add it to the home screen ("Add to Home screen" / "Install app"). When the home screen says "✅ Ready to use offline", it also opens without internet.
  • Only speech recognition and class sync need internet. Work done offline is not lost; it is sent when the connection returns.
  • Voice: Aktapokus speaks with the phone's own English voice. For offline speech the phone needs an English voice pack; if it is missing, the app shows how to install it on first launch.
  • Updates arrive by themselves. Just open the app while online; new content and fixes download automatically, no reinstalling needed. Only changed files are downloaded, so updates use very little mobile data.

Android app (APK)

  • An Android app called Aktapokus Kids English is ready and being tested; a Google Play release is planned.
  • It is the same as the web version: installed on the phone, opened from its icon, full screen without an address bar.
  • Because the content comes from the site, it updates itself; no reinstalling for every fix. Offline use works as in the web version.
  • Coming with the APK: a daily reminder notification at a time the parent chooses, and a screen to install the English voice pack with one tap.
  • To use it today without waiting for the APK: open the app on the phone and choose "Add to Home screen" in the browser menu.

Privacy (in short)

  • Progress stays on the child's own device.
  • When a child joins a class, only their name and progress numbers go to the teacher.
  • No email, phone number, location or voice recording is collected.

For educational organisations and volunteers

  • Free, nothing to install: Opens in the browser on shared tablets, in a library or a learning centre. Several children can use the same device with separate profiles.
  • For places with weak internet: Open the tablets once on Wi-Fi and leave them for a few minutes; after that they can be used offline in class.
  • Volunteer-friendly: Classroom mode and the printables (cards, bingo, worksheets) are designed so that a volunteer who is not an English teacher can also run a ready-made activity. The correct pronunciation is given with audio on every screen.
  • Continuing at home: The child carries on at home from where they left off; the family gets a weekly summary and sentences to say at home.
  • Status: The app is almost ready for release and everyday use. I have checked the content one by one against the source lists, but I don't want to spread it widely before an independent teacher or subject expert has looked at it. So a small pilot, or even just a quick look and a comment, would help me a lot; I fix things with every piece of feedback.

Try it in 5 minutes

  1. Open the app, create a profile and play the first episode of the Adventure.
  2. In the teacher panel, create an account and a test class, and join it with the code from your own phone.
  3. In the panel, press Sınıf modunu aç (Open classroom mode) and try the "Questions (teams)" activity.
