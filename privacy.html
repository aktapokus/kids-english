Aktapokus Word Safari - Privacy

English

  • We collect no personal data. No account, no photos, no location, no ads, no analytics, no third-party trackers.
  • Profile name, avatar and progress are stored only on your device (browser storage). They are never sent to any server. Clearing the app data deletes them.
  • The microphone is optional and only used in the pronunciation game if you allow it. Speech recognition is performed by your browser or device speech service (on Android/Chrome this is Google's service), so audio may be processed by that service under its own privacy policy. This app never receives, records or stores the audio. You can skip the game.
  • This app supports learning; it does not replace a teacher or a specialist. If you have concerns about a child's learning, please consult a professional.
  • There is no timer, ranking or penalty for wrong answers. Sessions are short and end on their own.
  • Pictures come from Pixabay (Pixabay Content License) and our own drawings. Aktapokus is an original character.
  • Found a mistake in a word, sentence or picture? Report a problem.
