Aktapokus Word Safari - Privacy

English

  • By default we collect no personal data. No account is required, no ads, no analytics, no third-party trackers.
  • Profile name, avatar and progress are stored only on your device (browser storage) unless you use one of the two optional features below. Clearing the app data deletes them.
  • Optional - Leaderboard: in the River Adventure game, you may choose to tap "Submit Score" to publish a nickname you type yourself and your score to a public leaderboard anyone can see. We ask you not to use your real name. This is entirely optional and only happens if you tap that button.
  • Optional - Class code: if your teacher gives you a class code, entering it in "My Aktapokus" sends your profile name and progress numbers (stars, streak, minutes, words) to that teacher's account only - not to anyone else, and not publicly visible. You can leave the class at any time from the same screen, which stops further updates. This exists so a teacher can follow their own students' progress, similar to a paper gradebook.
  • The microphone is optional and only used in the pronunciation game if you allow it. Speech recognition is performed by your browser or device speech service (on Android/Chrome this is Google's service), so audio may be processed by that service under its own privacy policy. This app never receives, records or stores the audio. You can skip the game.
  • This app supports learning; it does not replace a teacher or a specialist. If you have concerns about a child's learning, please consult a professional.
  • There is no timer or penalty for wrong answers. Sessions are short and end on their own.
  • Pictures come from Pixabay (Pixabay Content License) and our own drawings. Aktapokus is an original character.
  • Found a mistake, or a concern about the leaderboard/class features? Email us.

Konuşma tanıma tarayıcınızın ya da cihazınızın ses servisi tarafından yapılır (Android/Chrome'da bu Google servisidir); ses o servisin kendi gizlilik politikasına göre işlenebilir. Bu uygulama sesi almaz, kaydetmez, saklamaz. Oyunu atlayabilirsiniz.
